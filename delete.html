Delete your Chess Kingdom data

Chess Kingdom: Learn & Play (Android) · developer Iniya Apps

How to request deletion

  1. Open Chess Kingdom and go to Settings. Note your display name.
  2. Send an email to iniyaapps@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Chess Kingdom data" and your display name.
  3. We confirm by email and delete your online data within 30 days.

To erase the data stored on your phone straight away, open Settings → Reset progress in the app, or uninstall the app.

What is deleted

  • Your online profile: display name, avatar, optional gender and country, rating, coin balance and last-online time.
  • Your friends list, friend requests, game invites and block list.
  • The moves, scores and chat messages of your online games.
  • Your anonymous account ID and notification token.

What may be kept

  • Abuse reports you filed or that were filed about you may be kept for up to 90 days to protect other players, then deleted.
  • Advertising data is processed by Google AdMob under Google's own policy. You can reset your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

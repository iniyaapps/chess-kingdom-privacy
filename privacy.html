Privacy Policy

Chess Kingdom: Learn & Play (Android) · Last updated 14 October 2026

Chess Kingdom is a chess trainer and chess game published by Iniya Apps. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and how you can delete it.

Information we collect

  • Anonymous account ID. When you use online play, Firebase Authentication creates an anonymous ID for your device. We never ask for your email address, phone number or password.
  • Profile. The display name you type, your chosen avatar, your optional gender (male, female, or not given) and country, your in-app rating and coin balance, and the time you were last online.
  • Social data. Your friends list, friend requests, game invites and block list.
  • Game data. Moves, Puzzle Rush duel scores, results and short chat messages of online games, plus abuse reports you file.
  • Advertising data. Google AdMob may collect your advertising ID, IP address and device information to serve and measure ads.

How we use it

  • Your display name and rating are shown to opponents and friends.
  • Your gender choice is used only to honour the optional "same gender only" quick-match preference. It is never shown to other players and never used for advertising.
  • Game data keeps both players' boards in sync and powers rematches.
  • Chat messages are stored for a short time so that reports of abuse can be reviewed.

Offline features

Piece School, Puzzle Worlds, Checkmate Challenge, solo Puzzle Rush, games against the computer, stars, streaks and trophies are stored only on your device and never uploaded. When you use online features, your coin balance is shared so that quick match can pair players of a similar level.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads are rated for a general audience and are not targeted at children. You can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation in Android Settings → Google → Ads. Google's own policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Chat safety

Chat is limited to short messages filtered for offensive words. Any player can be reported or blocked from the game screen. Blocked players are never matched with you again. Reports are reviewed and repeat offenders lose access to online play.

Age

Chess Kingdom is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used online features, contact us and we will delete the data.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. Online data is stored with Google Firebase (Realtime Database and Authentication). Advertising data is processed by Google AdMob. No other third party receives your data.

Deleting your data

Choose Settings → Reset progress inside the app to erase everything stored on your device. To delete your online profile, friends and game history, email us with your display name and we will remove it within 30 days.
